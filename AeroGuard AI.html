How the simulator works and where it stops

Hover power comes from momentum theory: the thrust needed to carry the weight, rotor disc area, air density (temperature and elevation) and rotor and motor efficiency. Forward flight trims induced power slightly and drag grows with airspeed cubed. Wind is split into headwind and crosswind along the route at a fixed airspeed, so a headwind slows the way out and a tailwind speeds the way back; a round trip never cancels it fully. Battery energy is nominal capacity × battery health × cold-weather derating × 85% usable. This is a first-order go / no-go screen. It ignores gusts, climb profiles, spraying (payload that gets lighter) and manufacturer power curves, so calibrate it with real logs.
